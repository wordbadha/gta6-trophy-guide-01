Roadmap to the platinum

Three stages, in order. Stage 1 is the story; do the one missable trophy as you go, because it cannot be repeated.

  1. 1 Story first

    Play Chapters 1–4, the named missions, the five character finales and the final mission. Meditate at every spot you pass — Positive Mental Attitude is the only missable trophy in the game.

  2. 2 Leonida cleanup

    With the map open, sweep the collectibles and activities: cards, WAiNK spots, vehicles, areas, garages, races, ranges, stranger strands and the robbery set.

  3. 3 Mop-up & platinum

    The long grinds — 50 challenges, $250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent, 500 choices — then 100% completion and the platinum.

⚠ Only 1 missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots during the story. Everything else can be earned after the finale.

    How this guide is honest

    • Names, descriptions and grades are PlayStation's official list, exactly as published — 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 hidden.
    • Every guide is written for this entry in our own words and names its sources beside it (PowerPyx, PlanetGTA6, KeenGamer, OutputLag, GTAVibes, GTA Zone, PSNProfiles). A source is cited, never copied.
    • Guides that add steps beyond the official description are marked Details unconfirmed — confirm them against the cited guide before you grind. Story guides need no extra steps, so they carry no such mark.
    • The app is static files only. It loads nothing from the network and works with the network off.
